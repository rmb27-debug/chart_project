NYC Farmers' Market Access not Year-Round for All

By Rose Marina Boubion
New York City has some of the most vibrant farmers markets in the country, but access isn’t the same for all New Yorkers. As some locals will head to their neighborhood greenmarket to get their hands on the freshest yams for their Thanksgiving feasts, others will lose access entirely. This year, about one-fourth of farmers markets in every borough will remain open for the whole year, except in the Bronx, where all 23 farmers markets will close for the winter.
While some see a farmers market as a summer activity, these greenmarkets provide fresh produce to residents, and closing for the season may reduce healthy options available to New Yorkers. The Bronx in particular is a borough with a higher incidence of food insecurity than the city as a whole, with 22.6% of residents experiencing food insecurity, compared with a rate of 16.8% citywide.
